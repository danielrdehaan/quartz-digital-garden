# Alex

**Role**: Sound/Music Designer

## Project Overview

Produced all audio and music for Jellyvision's employee benefits onboarding system.

Alex is an interactive software platform that helps employees understand and choose their benefits packages. The platform uses conversational interfaces and engaging audio to make benefits education accessible and enjoyable.

## Responsibilities

- Complete audio production
- Music composition
- UI sound design
- Voice-over direction and integration
