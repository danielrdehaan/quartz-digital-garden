# T-Mobile: 5G&me Experience Center

**Role**: Sound/Music Designer

## Project Overview

Designed audio and music for a 4,000-square-foot immersive exhibition at T-Mobile US headquarters, working through Waveplant.

The 5G&me Experience Center showcases T-Mobile's 5G technology through interactive exhibits and immersive environments, demonstrating the potential of next-generation connectivity.

## Responsibilities

- Sound design for interactive exhibits
- Music composition for immersive spaces
- Audio system design and implementation

## Client

T-Mobile US (via Waveplant)
