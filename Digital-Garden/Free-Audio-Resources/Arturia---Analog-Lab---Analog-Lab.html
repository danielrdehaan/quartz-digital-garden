Analog Lab Play

Key sounds for creatives

Welcome to sound exploration!

Analog Lab Play is a free software instrument with a library of 100 essential, hard-hitting, mix-ready synths, keys, basses, pads, and more that’ll make you fall in love with music all over again - for free.

Analog Lab Play is a musical software that runs on computers only. Browse this page with your favorite computer and download it!

Hear the sound library

Making trap beats? Building the next hyperpop banger? Looking for that authentic retro vibe? Get the right sonic flavors for your style: check out the sounds of Analog Lab in action.

American Home Grand

Clean version of the American Home base model.

Jazz B-3 Organ

Classic and versatile B-3 through a Leslie, with a touch of reverb.

Classic Jun Keys

A bright but mellow sounding synth key/pad.

Sustained Strings

Classic string ensemble with loud sustains morphing into loud tremolos.

Deep Pluck

An ever-changing bass. Morph mix between truly realistic and synthetic.

Space Mallets

Mallets hitting wood bars. Arpeggiator & delay provide instant minimal music.

Brazen Lead

Hot overdriven lead.

Plucked Square Lead

A plucky and retro lead sound.

Book Of Memories

Smooth atmospheric keys with a long release. Mod wheel adds glide.

Arp Simplicity

Simple arpeggio with interesting dry/wet delay modulation to add subtle variation.

Berliner

Gentle percolating sequence with stacked saw waves and warm low pass filter sweep on the mod wheel.

Met Before Our Time

Epic and classy EP with an evolving spatial tail end. A seamless combo of the Jup-8 and Stage-73.

Chill Icon

(Victor Morello)

A chilled out yet upbeat modern vibe! Melodic elements and percussion come from Analog Lab Play.

Eté Indien

(Maxime Desormieres)

Instrumental hip-hop track with lo-fi inspirations at a tempo of 140BPM. All melodies and basses come from the 100 Analog Lab Play presets. Hats and percussion come from the 808 kit except the main kick and snare.

Haruto’s Remembrance

(Heather Fenoughty)

Nostalgic drama on flutes and vocals with expansive ambiences and a driving arpeggio bassline. Features Haruto’s Mystic Split and Vocce Choir over Teenage Fugue, Synthwave Split, Electric Wurli, Astral Sweep Pad, Mellow Memories, Atacama Ambience, Ambient Spectrum, Modular Rain, Met Before Our Time, American Home Grand and 80s Funk Bass.

Main Features

  • 100 Presets created by expert sound designers from VCollection instruments and Pigments
  • One simple interface with 4 macros: brightness, timbre, time, movement and 4 effects: 2 mod effects, delay, reverb
  • Updated browser to find sounds quickly: filter by type and instrument
  • Sound Store: 200+ Artist & Genre based sound banks
  • New paid and Free content added regularly
  • Automatic mapping of parameters for Arturia keyboards
  • Parameters can be mapped easily to other MIDI controllers

Platforms specifications

Windows

  • Win 10+ (64bit)
  • 4 GB RAM
  • 4 cores CPU, 3.4 GHz (4.0 GHz Turbo-boost)
  • 4GB free hard disk space
  • OpenGL 2.0 compatible GPU
  • ARM processors not supported on Windows

Apple

  • Mac OS 10.13+
  • 4 GB RAM
  • 4 cores CPU, 3.4 GHz (4.0 GHz Turbo-boost) or Apple Silicon CPU
  • 4GB free hard disk space
  • OpenGL 2.0 compatible GPU
